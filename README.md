## About Me

I'm a software engineer who likes problems where models meet the physical world: simulating complex systems, learning fast approximations of them, and turning messy sensor data into something people can act on. I've built hackathon projects end to end under tight deadlines, and I enjoy going deep on the details too, from reading primary regulatory text to validating parsers against real hardware output.

I'm looking for software engineering, ML/AI, and robotics roles where I can keep building systems that are tested, deployed, and useful.

## Interests

- **Surrogate modeling and simulation:** replacing slow simulators with fast neural approximations, and quantifying how much to trust them.
- **Robotics, perception, and sensing:** real-time tracking, multi-sensor fusion, and reliable data from physical hardware.
- **AI evaluation and reasoning:** benchmarks that measure how well models reason about hard scientific problems.
- **Health and life sciences:** epidemic modeling and epigenetic aging.
- **Energy and infrastructure:** stress-testing grid-connected systems before real faults do.
- **Tech for local communities:** practical tools that give small businesses clarity.
